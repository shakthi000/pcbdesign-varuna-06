VARUNA06 / REVISION B.2

Design notes

01 / PHYSICAL DESIGN

A compact, serviceable assembly

90 × 50 mm outline, two copper layers, 1.6 mm FR-4 and an ENIG finish. Four Ø3.2 mm mounting holes accommodate M3 fasteners. Screw terminals and internal JST-style headers keep the sensor harness replaceable.

02 / SIGNAL INTEGRITY

Separate functions, continuous ground

The analog front end stays away from the switching supply and coil driver. Short, paired EM receive paths feed a gain and low-pass stage, then the ADS1115. The bottom layer is reserved primarily for a continuous, via-stitched ground plane.

03 / ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION

Waterproof at the system level

Install the coated PCB inside a pressure-rated sealed housing with qualified bulkhead feedthroughs. The internal screw/JST connectors are not waterproof. Keep connector contacts and pressure interfaces clear of coating; verify the assembled enclosure’s ingress and depth rating.

04 / INTEGRATION

Designed around the tether

Protected 12 V input supplies 5 V and 3.3 V rails. RS-485 provides the tether data interface. Route the magnetometer away from transmitter fields, high-current loops and magnetic hardware; use the remote I²C sensor header where appropriate.

Concept visualization, not fabrication data. Verify the schematic, footprints, coil current and thermal limits, EM front-end bandwidth, ADS1115 sampling requirements, creepage, routing clearances and EMC. Complete DRC, calibration and pressure testing before manufacturing or deployment.
